# Decision Under Uncertainty Framework

## 1. Decisions
What can management choose?

## 2. Uncertainties
What outcomes are outside management control?

## 3. Probabilities
Use prior probabilities, empirical distributions, or scenario assumptions.

## 4. Consequences
Map each decision/outcome combination to payoff, cost, utility, or another objective.

## 5. Solve
Risk-neutral: expected monetary value.
Risk-averse: expected utility / certainty equivalent.
Information: compare expected value with and without information.
Bayesian: update probabilities after signals.
Simulation: generate outcome distributions when analytic enumeration is impractical.

## 6. Sensitivity
Identify the probability/payoff thresholds at which the preferred decision changes.

## 7. Communicate risk
Report distributions and downside/upside measures—not only averages.
