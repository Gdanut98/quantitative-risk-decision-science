# Interview One-Pager

## 30-second explanation
My quantitative-risk work focuses on decisions where uncertainty matters. I built decision trees, calculated EMV and value of information, updated probabilities with Bayes' theorem, modeled risk aversion with certainty equivalents, and used Monte Carlo simulation to generate outcome distributions.

## Best VOI story
Information is not automatically worth buying. I compare expected value with and without information, then compare that improvement with the information cost.

## Best risk story
Averages alone can hide downside risk. Simulation lets me show the full distribution, percentiles and probability of loss, while utility/certainty-equivalent methods incorporate risk preference into the decision itself.

## Resume connection
This extends forecasting and operational analytics experience into explicit uncertainty modeling and risk-aware decision support.
